### 🏭 What I do

I work in **industrial automation**, building **Python automations and data analysis** for manufacturing.

- ⚡ Automated internal reporting that used to take **~5 days of manual work** — it now runs in **under 10 minutes** with one click.
- 📊 Analyze customer **production data** to measure productivity and pinpoint **bottlenecks**, so decisions are based on evidence.
- 🔧 Background in **Mechatronics Engineering**: hardware, firmware and the data that comes out of them.
